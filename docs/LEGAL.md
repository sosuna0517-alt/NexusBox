# Legal

NexusBox is a frontend only. It does not and will not bundle:

- Emulator cores protected by third-party licenses unless the license permits it.
- Console BIOS/firmware images.
- Commercial game content.

Users must own the games they play and cores must be built/installed by the user
from their upstream sources. Emulation itself is legal; distribution of encrypted
system files and copyrighted ROMs is not.
